# Business Email Examples

## Meeting Rescheduling
**User Input:** "Email Dr. Smith to reschedule our meeting from Friday to next Monday"

**AI Output:**
```json
{
  "to": "dr.smith@company.com",
  "subject": "Rescheduling Our Meeting",
  "body": "Dear Dr. Smith, I hope this email finds you well. I'm writing to respectfully request if we could reschedule our meeting currently set for this Friday to next Monday at the same time. Please let me know if this works for your schedule. Best regards, [Your Name]"
}
